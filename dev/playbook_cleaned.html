Chapter Two

Search Candidates — All 8 Tabs

This is the screen you will use the most. Every search starts here. There are 8 tabs (filters) you can use. Once you know them, you can find any nurse in under a minute.

⚠ The #1 Rule — Always Search First
  • Search by email first. If nothing shows up, try the phone number. Then try the name. Only create a new profile if you find nothing at all
  • If you create a duplicate profile, it splits up the candidate's history. This causes problems for the whole team

ALL 8 FILTER TABS
TAB 1

📋 Profession / Specialty

Start your search here. Click ADD next to a job type (like RN). The button turns red — that means it's selected. Then pick a specialty from the right side. You'll see your search build at the bottom.

Example: PROFESSION [ RN IS CVICU ]
TAB 2

📜 Licenses

Pick the license type, country, and state. Set the state to match where the job is. Set "Expiry After" to a date past the job's end date. Always check that the license is Active.

Example: RN · USA · California · Active
TAB 3

🏥 Certifications

Every healthcare worker needs BLS. ICU nurses also need ACLS. Pediatric (children's) nurses need PALS. Set the expiry date after the job ends so JobDiva skips anyone whose certification will run out too soon.

Example: ACLS · active · expiry after assignment end
TAB 4

🎓 Qualification

This is about the candidate's degree. Use this if the client needs someone with a BSN or MSN degree. You can combine this with the Licenses tab when searching for Nurse Practitioners or CRNAs.

Example: BSN for magnet hospital contracts
TAB 5

✅ Require

List the skills the candidate MUST have. Each row is one skill, with the minimum years of experience needed. Check the "Recent" box if you want someone who has worked at the bedside in the last 12 months.

Example: ICU RN · Years ≥ 2 · Recent ✓
TAB 6

❌ Exclude

This removes any candidate who matches what you type here. Be careful — it removes a lot of people, so only use it when you're sure.

Example: "New Grad" from CVOR travel searches
TAB 7

🏷 Attribute

This searches for special tags that recruiters add to candidates, like "Compact License," "Top Candidate," or "Do Not Place." This is a quick way to find candidates who are already checked and approved.

Example: search tag "Compact License"
TAB 8

💼 Title

This searches by job title. It's helpful for roles that aren't in the main Profession list, like CT Tech, Echo Tech, or Surgical Tech.

Example: "Cardiac Sonographer"
PANEL

📍 Right Side Panel

This panel stays on screen no matter which tab you're on. You can set the State, Zip code, search radius, Pay Range, and the date the candidate is available. "w/o Mapping" turns off JobDiva's auto-matching of similar words.

If you turn on "w/o Mapping", searching "RN" will NOT also find "Registered Nurse"

Chapter Four

New Candidate — 4 Entry Methods

How to add a new person to JobDiva — and how to avoid creating the same profile twice.

🔴 Always Search First, Before Creating a New Profile
  • Search by email, then phone, then name. Only create a new profile if nothing comes up
  • If you make a duplicate, it splits up their history and your teammates won't see important notes
Step 1 — Look Up a Candidate First
🔍 What the "Look up a candidate" Tab Does
  • When you click New Candidate, the screen always opens on the "Look up a candidate" tab first — this is on purpose, not a mistake
  • This tab lets you search for a person who may already exist in JobDiva, using fields like email, phone number, or name
  • Fill in at least one field and click the search button. If a match is found, open their existing record instead of creating a new one
  • Only switch to the "Create a candidate" tab (shown below) if your search returns zero results
Screenshot — Create a Candidate: All 4 Methods
📸 Real Screenshot — Create candidate tab · Upload / Email / Paste / Register Without Resume
Look up a candidate / Create a candidate tabs · Create candidate panel · Upload Resume · Resume Source* · Browse · Upload
📌 At the top, you'll see 4 options: Upload, Email, Paste, and Register Without Resume. "Upload" is picked by default. You MUST also pick a Resume Source before uploading. Click Browse to choose a file. Files must be under 5 MB.
📁

1 · Upload

You have the resume file. Click Browse, pick the file (.doc, .docx, or .pdf — under 5MB), choose a Resume Source, then click Upload. JobDiva fills in the details for you.

After uploading, double check the name, email, and phone are correct
📨

2 · Email

If a candidate emails you their resume, just forward it to your JobDiva email address. JobDiva will create their profile automatically in a few minutes.

Great for busy days with lots of resumes coming in
📋

3 · Paste

If you have resume text from LinkedIn or a website, click "Paste," paste the text in, and click Submit. JobDiva will organize the information for you.

Good for when you find someone on LinkedIn but have no file
📝

4 · Register Without Resume

If you talked to someone on the phone but they have no resume yet, use this. It creates a basic profile with their name, phone, and email. You can add their resume later.

Set a reminder to get their resume within 24 hours
💡 Example — Mitchell Calls You

Mitchell, a nurse, calls you after seeing your post on LinkedIn. Before you create a new profile, type his phone number 951-300-8582 in Look Up. You find he's already in the system from a job fair. Open his profile, add a note about this call, and talk to him. No duplicate created — and his history is saved.

⌨ Resume Source — You Must Pick One
  • Job Board — sites like Indeed, Monster, or CareerBuilder
  • LinkedIn — you messaged them or they messaged you
  • Referral — someone else suggested this person
  • Career Site — they applied on HonorVet's website
  • Database — they were already in JobDiva and you're reaching out again
  • Event / Job Fair — you met them in person

DivaMatch & The Harvester

Two tools that work for you all day and night — one finds the best job matches, the other finds new candidates automatically.

How To Read DivaMatch Scores

DivaMatch looks at every open job and gives it a score based on how well it fits this candidate — their skills, specialty, location, pay, and shift. A higher score means a better match.

  • →Score 100 — a perfect match! Mention this job first when you call
  • →Score 70 and up — a strong match. Maybe the pay or shift is a little different, but still worth talking about
  • →Score 50 to 69 — an okay match. Check what doesn't fit before you bring it up
  • →Below 50 — not a great match. Double-check everything before mentioning it to the candidate
  • →Email Selected Jobs — check the boxes next to jobs and email them all to the candidate at once
  • →Job Filters (on the right) — narrow down jobs by date, zip code, experience needed, and more
💡 Example — Calling Jellie

Before you call, sort by Highest Score. The top job (score 100) is a CVOR Tech role in Wisconsin, day shift, paying $2,660 a week, for 13 weeks. You can simply say: "Hi Jellie, I have a CVOR job in Wisconsin, day shift, $2,660 a week. Want to hear more?" DivaMatch basically wrote your opening line for you!

THE HARVESTER — WORKS FOR YOU 24/7
🌐

What the Harvester Does

This runs by itself on every open job — as long as the Qualifications tab is filled in. It searches job boards all day and night, even while you sleep, and finds new matching candidates for you.

If the status says "On Schedule," it's working right now
⛔

"Missing Criteria" Means It's Turned Off

If you see "Missing Criteria," the Harvester is NOT working for that job — zero new candidates. To fix it: open the job, go to Qualifications, add at least one Required Skill, make sure the location is filled in, then click Save. That's all it takes to turn it back on.

Check your open jobs every week — this is an easy thing to miss
🔔

Agent Search — Get Alerts Automatically

Instead of running the same search over and over, save it as an "Agent." JobDiva will alert you whenever a new matching candidate is added. Great for hard-to-fill jobs.

Example: Save "CVOR RN with CA license and active ACLS" as an Agent

Chapter Six

Compliance & Submittal — 9 Steps

These are 9 steps you must follow every time, for every candidate, before you submit them to a job. No skipping steps.

⚠ Why You Can't Skip Any Step
  • Skipping the license check could put a patient at risk and get HonorVet in trouble
  • Skipping the OIG check breaks the rules for government-funded hospitals
  • Skipping the Pipeline check can mean submitting someone twice — this looks bad for HonorVet
  • Skipping RTR means the candidate didn't agree to be submitted — that's a problem
1
Step 1: Check If They Qualify
Compare their resume to the job. Does their specialty match? Have they worked recently? Do they have enough years of experience? If it doesn't look right on paper, move to the next candidate.
2
Step 2: Check Their License
Go to the Licenses tab. Make sure: the license type matches the job, the state matches (or they have a compact license), it says Active, and it won't expire during the job. Write down the license number. Example: Jellie's RN license #599289, California, Active, expires Feb 2028 ✓
3
Step 3: Check Their Certifications
Every healthcare worker needs BLS. ICU, CVOR, and critical care also need ACLS. Children's units need PALS. Make sure nothing expires during the job. Note: Jellie shows 0 certifications — bring this up before submitting her for an ICU job.
4
Step 4: Check OIG and SAM Lists
Check the candidate on oig.hhs.gov and SAM.gov — these are government lists. Write the result in Notes, like: "OIG clear 06/05/2026 — [your initials]". Hospitals that get government funding cannot hire anyone on these lists.
5
Step 5: Check Background and Vaccines
Make sure their background check is recent (usually within the last 12 months). Check that their Flu, Hepatitis B, MMR, and TB results meet what THIS client wants — every client is a bit different. Check the job notes for details.
6
Step 6: Call Them — Confirm Everything
Call them. Make sure they: (1) actually want this job and facility, (2) can start on the right date, (3) are okay with the shift, and (4) the pay works for them. Get a clear "yes" on all four. Example: "Jellie, this is a CVOR job in Wisconsin, day shift, $2,660 a week — does that work for you?"
7
Step 7: Get Right to Represent (RTR)
Ask them clearly if it's okay to submit them to this client. Once they say yes, write it down right away in Notes: "RTR given 06/05/2026 for Aya Healthcare — CVOR WI · [your name]"
8
Step 8: Make Sure They're Not Already Submitted
Open the candidate's Activities or Pipeline tab. Make sure no one — including you — has already submitted them to this client. If they have, talk to that recruiter. Don't submit them again.
9
Step 9: Submit and Set a Reminder
Write a short note about the call. Attach the resume in HonorVet's format. Click Submit. Then set a reminder to follow up in 48 hours. The steps after this are: Submitted → Client Review → Interview → Offer → Placed.
